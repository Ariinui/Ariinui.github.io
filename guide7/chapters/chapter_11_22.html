Evidence #22: Isaiah/Micah in 3 Nephi

Analysis of Christ’s quotations of Isaiah and Micah (3 Nephi 20:10–23:5) demonstrates that they are thematically linked and were likely integrated into Christ’s sermon at certain locations to create a chiastic structure.
