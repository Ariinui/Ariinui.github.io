Evidence #52: Alma’s Consistent Conversion Accounts

The similarities and dissimilarities in the three accounts of Alma’s conversion are consistent with their having been written by a single individual, but in different settings and at different stages of his life.

Evidence #268: Wordplay on Zoram

When interpreted from a Semitic background, the name Zoram can plausibly denote one who is “high” or “exalted.” In what appears to be intentional wordplay, Book of Mormon authors repeatedly connect this name with these concepts.
